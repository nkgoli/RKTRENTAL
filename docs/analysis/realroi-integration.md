## Units, timing, missing values and boundedness

- All monetary inputs/outputs are **currency units**, e.g. dollars, not cents. One currency per analysis; no FX conversion. Convert RocketRental integer cents by dividing by 100 exactly once at the boundary; convert Prisma Decimal through the application's money helper/explicit numeric conversion. Do not pass Decimal instances as financial numbers.
- Financial input percentages are **percentage points**: interestRate 7 means 7%, vacancyRate 5 means 5%. Core summary return ratios and IRRs are **fractions**: 0.07 means 7%. `discountRate` is also a fraction. Benchmark CAGR/yield/volatility and comparison returns are percentage points. Gross annual yield can be displayed as `summaryMetrics.grossPotentialIncome / summaryMetrics.purchasePrice`; it is distinct from monthly `rentToPrice`.
- Rent/other income, HOA and utilities inputs are monthly. Tax, insurance, maintenance, CapEx reserve, management, leasing and other expenses are annual. Explicit dollar overrides for maintenance/CapEx/management take precedence over percentage estimates, including explicit zero.
- Inputs are finite and bounded to +/-10 billion before schema constraints; holding/loan/amortization terms are at most 50 years. Source percentage limits apply (growth -50..100, vacancy/interest 0..100, selling costs 0..30). Output outside safe whole-cent precision (`MAX_SAFE_INTEGER / 100`) throws. Invalid, missing, null and out-of-range assumptions throw rather than produce a result using invented facts. The caller should render validation errors and preserve drafts.
- `dscr` is null for cash purchases; zero gross income makes OER/GRM/break-even null; zero invested equity makes cash-on-cash/equity multiple/return percentage/profitability index null. `unavailable` provides reasons. After-tax metrics are null when tax assumptions are absent. Unrecovered payback and ambiguous/noncomputable IRRs remain null; never coerce null to zero for charts or rankings.
- Annual growth starts in year two; appreciation compounds over the hold from ARV when explicitly supplied, otherwise purchase price. Month labels start on `startDate`; debt payments occur one month later. UTC month arithmetic clamps month ends (Jan 31 → Feb 28), independent of local timezone/DST. Acquisition outflow is time zero, operating cash flow aggregated at each year end, and sale added once at final year end. `computedAt` echoes the supplied start date and `computeTimeMs` is zero for reproducibility; they are model metadata, not an audit timestamp.
- Source expense projections retain a fixed year-one dollar baseline (including percentage-derived expenses) grown by annualExpenseGrowth. They do not recompute management/maintenance percentages against later rent. Break-even holds that modeled expense baseline fixed and can exceed 100%; it is not a variable-cost optimization. No rent-by-lease schedule, adjustable-rate debt, refinancing, rate resets, extra payments or inflation-adjusted returns are modeled.
- Monthly rounded cash flows are summed into annual series. Analytic year-one summary expenses/NOI can differ from the sum of rounded monthly amounts by a few cents. Do not add summary and annual figures together.

## Demonstrated corrections from pinned source

| Source behavior                                                                                                                                               | Adaptation and independent evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Cash required used downPaymentPercent even when explicit loanAmount overrode debt.                                                                            | Acquisition equity is price minus actual loan amount plus acquisition costs/points/fees. A 100,000 price and 60,000 explicit loan require 40,000 before fees, regardless of the percent field. Explicit loan wins; loan exceeding price is rejected.                                                                                 |
| Balloon balance could vanish beyond loan term; final rounding could leave residue.                                                                            | Remaining principal is paid in the maturity month's cash flow, then debt is zero. A 60,000 zero-rate, 30-year amortization/one-year term repays exactly 60,000, including the final 58,166.63 balloon. No assumed refinance or forgiven debt. Year-one summary debt sums actual schedule payments.                                   |
| Initial equity included closing/transaction costs; appreciation component subtracted rehab again.                                                             | Equity decomposition now starts with purchase equity, with ARV-minus-price instant appreciation; closing costs are invested cash, not market equity. Equity equals value minus actual debt.                                                                                                                                          |
| Unlevered IRR omitted acquisition costs.                                                                                                                      | Includes closing, rehab and furnishing costs, but excludes financing points/fees. Cash purchase with 3,500 acquisition costs and 110,800 returned has IRR `110800/103500-1`.                                                                                                                                                         |
| Source included CapEx reserve in operating expenses/NOI.                                                                                                      | CapEx reserve is shown separately in expenseBreakdown and deducted from spendable cash flow, not NOI or taxable operating income. 12,000 rent − 1,200 operating cost gives 10,800 NOI; a further 1,200 reserve leaves 9,600 cash flow. Annual reserve at each projection point is `noi - debtService - cashFlow` (monthly rounding). |
| IRR could pick one of several roots; zero denominators often returned 0 or Infinity; break-even was capped at 100%.                                           | Conservative public IRR rejects `[-100,230,-132]` (10% and 20% roots). Undefined ratios return null. Negative cash flow remains negative; 24,000 cost against 12,000 rent exposes 200% required occupancy.                                                                                                                           |
| Tax pipeline ignored includeTaxAnalysis, required depreciation to enable all tax analysis, and used equity/net-of-debt proceeds as tax basis.                 | Public tax model works with or without depreciation. Sale gain uses net asset sale price minus adjusted asset basis, independent of debt. A 20,000 asset gain has 3,000 sale tax at the editable 15% assumption, under cash or debt financing.                                                                                       |
| Accumulated depreciation lookup could return zero after the depreciation schedule ended.                                                                      | Public tax analysis accumulates each year's depreciation, retaining the full accumulated amount after exhaustion (30-year hold reference).                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Comparison valued final property equity without selling costs, invented property volatility/yield, extrapolated missing horizons, and ignored dividend flags. | Public comparison uses actual net liquidation proceeds plus cumulative operating cash flows. No inferred property risk, no extrapolation, no second yield addition. Benchmark CAGR is total return with reinvested distributions; property cash flows are held without reinvestment.                                                 |
| Clock, local month overflow and performance timing made results nondeterministic.                                                                             | Explicit public date/rate options and UTC clamping; repeated analyses deep-equal in tests.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

Independent tests also cover PMT against the annuity formula, first-month interest/principal, closed-form balance within rounding tolerance, zero rates, early payoff, losses, acquisition costs, sale override, appreciation, growth, zero equity/income, invalid numbers/terms/rates, 50-year IRR, scenario immutability and benchmark total return without double-counted dividends.

## Tax and comparison limitations

Tax options are editable scenario assumptions, not tax advice or a jurisdictional compliance engine. Federal and state income rates are additive and bounded to a combined 100%; recapture, capital-gains rate, land allocation, depreciation life/enabled state and immediate loss benefit are all explicit. Straight-line depreciation retains the source's annual model and final fractional year. No acquisition mid-month convention, passive-loss carryforwards, deduction limits, capital improvement schedules, furnishings depreciation, state sale taxes, short-term gains switching or individual tax treatment. Cost basis simplification is purchase + closing + rehab; furnishing costs affect cash invested but not real-estate tax basis. Negative sale gains generate no automatic tax refund. CapEx reserve is treated as cash set aside, never assumed deductible; actual capital improvements need a future basis/depreciation model. Terminal reserve recovery is not assumed.

Upstream `benchmarks/data/market-data.ts` contains **dated source assumptions through 2024**, not fresh quotes or verified market data: S&P 500 10.5%, Nasdaq 100 12.5%, bonds 5%, REITs 9.5%, savings 4.5%, custom 8%. Their source labels and yields are historical examples. The public API has no default benchmark selection or rate; callers provide an editable `BenchmarkData` and source/as-of label. A total-return CAGR already includes distributions. Source dividend inclusion/reinvestment flags were unused; no such false controls are exposed. No volatility/Sharpe prediction is supplied. Comparison property values before final sale are equity plus cumulative cash flow; final point includes sale costs/override. Annualized wealth return is null for negative final wealth and is distinct from timing-aware IRR. Do not sum property IRRs or average cap rates across a portfolio; aggregate money flows before calculating portfolio returns.

## Current RocketRental mapping and next persistence boundary

Read `prisma/schema.prisma`, `src/lib/returns/core.ts`, `src/lib/returns/queries.ts`, and the current returns/property-returns view composition. Recorded Returns aggregate succeeded Payment amounts by paidAt and property through lease → unit; expenses are Expense rows by occurredOn/category. Scheduled rent sums current ACTIVE Lease.monthlyRent. Existing Returns use integer cents internally, recorded annualDebtService prorated over the selected reporting period, and a labeled 3% appreciation assumption. These are not mortgage projections or a calibrated forecast dataset. Current payment aggregation does not separately classify rent/deposit/other income; do not present it as a clean rent observation without reconciliation.
